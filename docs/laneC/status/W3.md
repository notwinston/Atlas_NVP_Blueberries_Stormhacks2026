## Seams for the other packages and lanes (who calls what)

- **Director (P2-C01):** create one `ActionMap`; `ActionMapDriver(input, map)` after the input tick; subscribe
  `map.Fired` to `ExhibitMenu.OnAction` and `HomeReturn.OnAction`. A beat sets `map.ClickRole`
  (Confirm, Dial for beat 5, Gluing in S1), `map.DoubleRole` (Mode for beats 7 and 8, RotateStep in S6),
  `map.HoldSweeps` (S6), `map.Q` (after every dial change) and `map.SetLive(...)`. Beat 10 calls `HomeReturn.Begin()` and
  `HomeReturn.Tick(dt, kernel.Pose, headFloor)` each frame. The chip: `CaptionChip.Attach(spaceHost)`; beat 8 calls
  `CaptionChip.ShowText(CaptionTexts.ForMode(1 or 2))`; the dial calls `CaptionChip.SetDial(q)`.
- **Start button:** lane D's `SpaceActionStep` (step 8b) polls `Pressed(SpaceMenu)` for the same press that
  `ActionMap` already saw in the input tick. During the Playground the director must set
  `SpaceActions.OpenSpaceMenu = null` (a settable property; nothing is edited in lane D) so that only `ActionMap` drives the
  menu. Setting it to `exhibitMenu.Open` would reopen the menu in the same frame that Start closed it (tested in
  `StartOpensAndClosesTheMenuWithSpaceActionsWiredToo`). The same step also polls a dwell on the board
  (`SpaceMenu` gaze target), which the null seam covers too.
- **Return Home and SpaceActions (cross-lane request to lane D):** `SpaceActions.Do(ReturnHome)` calls
  `host.ReturnHome()` at once on B held or a dwell on the home beacon, a fade switch with no kappa ramp. `HomeReturn`
  tolerates this (a return already done or under way counts as asked) but the 4 s ramp of beat 10 is skipped on those
  two paths. Request to lane D: a hook (for example `Func<bool> ClaimReturnHome` on `SpaceActions`) that lets the Playground
  claim ReturnHome during the run. Until then the triple click of the lever is the only path that always ramps.
- **Beat 9 / sign placement:** `ExhibitMenu.PlaceSigns(room, pgGazeTargets)` once the room is known. The views take the
  HUD parent and the material pool (`CaptionChipView.Create`, `EquationCardView.Create`) or the room root
  (`ExhibitMenuView.Create`) and want `Update` once a frame.
- **Lane A:** `IEquationSource` for S1, S5, S6 registered with `EquationSources.Register` (P2-A03); the Slicer toybox
  sets `ExhibitMenu.SlicerSlot` (`IExhibitSlot`: `Ready`, `Launch()`); S6's `Axis(Sweep)` and `Axis(Twist)` stay on the
  input service, and the lever's sweep is `ActionMap.LeverSweep`. The Slicer should `SetLive(PgAction.Gluing, false)`.
- **Lane B (bound at integration, nothing of lane B is referenced):** `IWalkReadout` over `LapWalk` (Armed, Q, Corners,
  Closed, DistanceToFlagM, WorldTurnDeg) plus side s and L/R; `ICellsReadout` over `AlignedCellsModule` (cell side `g`,
  `Crossings`); `ICurvatureRamp` over the dial (kappa ramp); `IFloorProjection` for the real kernel.
  Stand-ins today: `FlatCurvatureRamp`, `FlatFloorProjection(IWalkerKernel)`.

## Deviations and derived values (to be confirmed)

1. `Atlas.Blades.Playground.asmdef` gained `Unity.InputSystem` (the lever's touch) and `Unity.Mathematics` (RoomModel
   and gaze types). If another package edits the same lines, keep both.
2. **Lever.** The input service fires `Confirm` on every lever touch (P1-C10 section 13.4); the plan's lever means a
   different action in each context. `ActionMap` therefore drops the service's `InputSource.Lever` firings and reads
   the touch itself (`ActionMapDriver.LeverDown`). Lever timings are provisional (open item 10): double/triple window
   0.30 s, long press 0.60 s, hold-to-sweep after 0.25 s. While Return Home is live a single click is held back by the
   0.30 s window (it has to tell a click from a triple click); Return Home is live by default in every beat, so that
   latency is the default for every lever click, and a beat that does not offer Return Home calls
   `SetLive(PgAction.ReturnHome, false)` to drop it. To be tuned on the phone in the headset. A touch cut short by a context
   change (the menu opening or closing under a held finger) is spent: it makes no second gesture and its release is silent.
3. A hold sweeps w and each hold goes the other way (the plan says only "hold to sweep"); derived.
4. **Chip texts.** S1 to S6 are the rules of section 10.1.3, verbatim. S0 ("Your room, rebuilt 1:1. Flat.") and the
   S2 texts for q = 4 and q = 6 are derived (a flat dial never says curved, rule of section 10.1.4); q = 3 reuses
   S4's rule. Chip stays 12 s (derived) then fades; the walker can `Dismiss()`.
5. **Equation cards.** S0 card is the flat counterpart (side s, angle sum 180 deg, distance from the flag, world turned).
   S3 uses the exact `L_eff = g / sqrt(2 pi / 5) = 0.892 g` and prints it as "L_eff = 0.89 g". The refresh rate is
   15 Hz (the plan asks for at least 10 Hz).
6. **Events.** `HomeReturn` logs `pg.home {wall_dev_m}` through `IEventLog`. The plan lists no event for it, but the
   acceptance number (0.05 m) needs a record. Nothing else adds an event name. Pending lead approval; the Station and
   dashboard read it as a free-form Appendix B event (name `pg.home`, one numeric field `wall_dev_m`, metres).
7. **Home registration check** is "the kernel's idea of where the walker stands equals the head's floor point" turned
   into a wall deviation (translation plus yaw about the head); for the flat kernel `FlatFloorProjection` decodes the
   cell and offset. Lane B's tiling needs its own `IFloorProjection`.
8. The Return Home ramp waits for `ICurvatureRamp.IsFlat` (at most 60 s, derived: lane B's dial ramps only while the
   walker stands still), then calls `ISpaceHost.ReturnHome()` (SpaceLab already does fade, walker reset to the floor and
   Home registered again); it fails after 10 s from the moment kappa is flat without Home running.
9. **What the 0.05 m test proves.** The headless test checks the geometry of the measurement (`HomeCheck`) against a
   flat kernel whose pose agrees with the head by construction. It does not prove the registration; that is the tape
   check on the phone, still pending below.
10. The S2 card's laps are `Corners / Q`, with the readout's own Q (lane B's lap closes at Q corners).
