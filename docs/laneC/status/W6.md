## Deviations and notes

1. The headless result is identical for each built space by construction: Bump Guard reads the physical pose and the
   physical scan and nothing from a space. The document says so; the rows that can differ per space (frame time while
   the cue plays, cue over the space's look, haptic, registration) are device rows.
2. The device approach set is ten wall approaches (the row's "10 of 10"), not the 20 of section 11.4.5 (which includes
   chair and stool and belongs to G5). The 20 stay in `BumpGuardReplayTests`.
3. The recorded wall approach stops 0.3 m short of the wall, so contact never happens. Lead is computed from the WARN
   position to `clear() = 0` (wall less 5 cm) at the log's 1.0 m/s.
4. The run sheet proposes one input per run (gamepad, gaze, lever); it also covers the `P2-C07` row. The pairing of
   walkers is a proposal. The SEQ is asked twice (S0 and S2) because the plan says one question per use case.
5. `check_run.py` reads `pg.beat` as `data.beat` (1 to 10) or `data.name`; the payload is not fixed in Appendix B.
6. The run needs things not on this branch: `PlaygroundDirector` and `Beats.asset` (`P2-C01`), lane B's spaces and dial,
   the Console SEQ field and the link carrying FMS and SEQ back to the phone (lane D), how the Spectator shows a beat.
   Listed in the document under "Seams to confirm".
7. Verify used the git-excluded stand-ins for `RoomStore.cs` and `ScanFailure.cs` (see STATUS, which also names the third, `RoomModelBuilder.cs`). They are not committed.
