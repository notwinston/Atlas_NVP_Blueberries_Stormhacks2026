# Recordings

Recorded walks and hand movements for replay in headless tests and, for walks, in the Editor (contract README F8):
`walk_*.poselog` are pose logs and `hands_*.handlog` are hand logs.
There are no room files here: the rooms of every test and replay are the five fixtures `sample_3x3`, `sample_L_3x4`,
`sample_4x5_table`, `budget_room` and `tiny_standing` in `ATLAS/Assets/Atlas/World/Resources/AtlasRooms/`
(`RoomFixtures.LoadFromProject(name)`; generated by `tools/rooms/make_fixtures.py`). No camera frame is ever stored here.

## Pose logs

The format is in `docs/contracts/lane-c-room.md` section 7.6: a text file that starts `ATLAS-POSELOG 1` and a header
(`room`, `frame`, `created`, `device`, then `data`), followed by one raw head pose per line,
`t x y z qx qy qz qw s`, with `s` = 0 not tracking, 1 limited, 2 normal. The codec is `Atlas.World.PoseLog`;
`Atlas.Scan.PoseLogReplaySource` plays a log as the head, and `Atlas.Scan.PoseLogRecorder` writes one on the
phone when `pose.record` is on (to `persistentDataPath/logs/`).

A pose log recorded from a person is motion data of that person. Commit one only with their agreement.

## Sample walks

The logs below are synthetic. They are in the room frame of the fixture `sample_3x3` (a 3 x 3 m room,
free rectangle 2.4 x 2.4 m, lap square 2.0 m), at 60 samples a second and an eye height of 1.6 m.

| File | Walk | Length |
|---|---|---|
| `walk_lap_sample_3x3.poselog` | One lap of the lap square at 0.5 m/s, from the corner (-1, -1) along +Z, then +X, -Z and -X, looking along the path and turning over the last 0.2 m of each side | 16 s, 961 samples |
| `walk_wall_approach.poselog` | Straight from (0, -1) towards the wall at z = 1.5 at 1.0 m/s, stopping 0.3 m short of it, then standing for one second | 3.2 s, 193 samples |
| `walk_jump_and_loss.poselog` | Along +X from (-1, 0) at 0.5 m/s. At sample 90 (1.5 s) the pose jumps 0.5 m along +Z and stays there, as after a relocalisation. From sample 150 (2.5 s) tracking is limited for one second while the pose keeps moving | 4 s, 241 samples |
| `walk_lap5_3x3.poselog` | Five sides of the lap square at 0.7 m/s, turning left: from the corner (-1, -1) along +X, then +Z, -X, -Z and +X again, ending at (1, -1), with a 0.5 s stop at each corner and the head turned along each side. In a q = 5 tiling the five sides close a loop (kernel tests) | 16.7 s, 1,005 samples |
| `walk_still_60s.poselog` | Standing at the origin for one minute with up to 0.5 mm of noise on each axis | 60 s, 3,600 samples |

The first three are the output of `SampleWalks` in `ATLAS/Assets/Atlas/Tests/Scan/Pose/`, and the test
`PoseLogTests.RecordingsFile_IsTheGeneratorsOutput` compares the files with it. To regenerate them after a change
to the generator:

```sh
ATLAS_WRITE_RECORDINGS=1 python3 tools/headless/verify.py test Atlas.Scan.Tests --filter "FullyQualifiedName~PoseLogTests"
```

`walk_lap5_3x3.poselog` and `walk_still_60s.poselog` are the walks of lane B's kernel and curved-space tests
(`Tests/CurvedWalk/`, `Tests/Tiles/TileLapTests.cs`, and the five-corner lap of `Tests/App/E2E/`). They were written
by a lane B script that no longer exists, then converted to this format (header `room sample_3x3`, `frame room`,
`device placeholder`; the tracking column was remapped to 2 = normal, the same samples in the same order), so they
have no generator: they are kept as they are, and the tests' expected numbers depend on them. `PoseLogTests` compares the first three with their generator; nothing
does that for these two, and adding them to `SampleWalks` would give them one.

## Replaying a walk in the Editor

With the room `sample_3x3` active (`RoomFixtures.Load`, or `-atlasRoom sample_3x3`), hand the log to the pose systems:

```csharp
var pose = AtlasServices.Get<Atlas.Scan.ArPoseSystems>();
pose.StartReplay("../recordings/walk_lap_sample_3x3.poselog", loop: true, out string error);
// ... and pose.StopReplay() returns to the keyboard walker or the AR camera.
```

## Hand logs

A hand log is what the phone's hand detector said, detection by detection: joints and depths, never a picture. The
codec is `Atlas.Hands.HandsLog` (`HandsLogWriter` and `HandsLogReader`, in
`ATLAS/Assets/Atlas/Hands/Sources/HandsLog.cs`); the design is `documents/ATLAS_Hands_Design.md` section 4.5. A file
is the text line `ATLAS-HANDLOG 1` and a line feed (16 bytes), then one binary record for every detection,
little-endian, 2,748 bytes each:

| Bytes | Field |
|---|---|
| 8 | `time`: a double, seconds on the app clock at the poll, counted from the first record of the file |
| 12 | The head's position, three floats, in the frame that polled the detection (the room frame while a room is active, the AR session's frame otherwise) |
| 16 | The head's rotation, four floats `x y z w` |
| 2,712 | `Atlas.Hands.HandsDetection`, byte for byte as the native plugin lays it out (`ATLAS/Assets/Plugins/iOS/AtlasHands.swift`): the camera frame's time and the poll's time on the detector's own clock, the picture and depth sizes, the intrinsics, the camera's pose in the AR session's frame, and for up to two hands the 21 joints (picture pixels, confidence and the LiDAR depth under each) and up to 64 palm depth samples |

A record holds joint positions and depths and no image (plan rule 10): the plugin lets the picture go before it
returns a result. A record is a copy of the native struct, so a change to the layout of `HandsDetection` changes the
format: `HandsLog.FormatVersion` is raised and the sample logs below are written again. The reader refuses a file with
another header or version, and a record whose time is not a number or goes back, whose detection version is not 1 or
whose hand count is over 2, and its message names the record. A file that ends inside a record (a phone that died while
writing) is read as far as its last whole record.

A hand log recorded from a person is motion data of that person. Commit one only with their agreement.

**Recording.** With the developer parameter `hands.record` on (the Console, or the menu's Parameters page) the hands'
runtime opens `hands_<UTC time>.handlog` in `persistentDataPath/logs/`, for example `hands_20261004T071406Z.handlog`,
and closes it when the parameter goes off or the app stops. A record is added for each detection of a running hands
session (the gate of the design's section 3.5: headset view, no gamepad, `hands.enabled` on, a source, the head from
the AR camera, a quality tier below Serious): with the parameter on and no session the file holds its header and
nothing else. The frame only copies a record into a ring of 256; a thread of the recorder's
own writes the file, so storage never holds the frame up, and a record is dropped, and counted, only when the writer is
a whole ring behind (`Atlas.Hands.HandsLogRecorder`).

**Replaying.** `Atlas.Hands.ReplayHandsSource` is an `IHandsSource` that plays a log against the clock it is given:

```csharp
var source = Atlas.Hands.ReplayHandsSource.FromFile("../recordings/hands_wave.handlog", loop: true);
// A test or a script gives it to the hands: new Atlas.Hands.HandsRuntime(new Atlas.Hands.HandsDeps { Source = source, ... })
```

It hands each record out once, when the time given to `TryRead`, counted from the first read, reaches the record's
time; when a frame is slower than the log it gives the newest record that is due and skips the ones between. With
`loop: true` the log starts again at its end, and the detector's clock and the sequence number are carried on from lap
to lap so that they keep rising. `HeadPosition` and `HeadRotation` give the head pose recorded with the record last
handed out. Nothing in the app chooses a replay source yet: there is no parameter or start option for it, as
`pose.source` is for pose logs, so the headless tests and scripts build the runtime with it. In the Editor the hands
come from `EditorHandsSource`, which mouse and keys drive while `hands.editor` is on
(`docs/verification/UNITY_SIDE_BRIEF.md`, step 3i).

## Sample hand logs

The three logs below are synthetic: a perfect detector and a perfect LiDAR, through a made-up 1280 x 720 camera (a
focal length of 1,000 px, a 256 x 144 depth image), 30 detections a second, each polled 80 ms after its frame was
captured, with no noise (a test that wants noise adds it). The detector's clock reads 5,000 s plus the log's time. The
head rests at (0, 1.6, 0) looking along +Z (the AR session's frame and the room frame are the same), except in
`hands_wave`, and the template hand (`HandModel`) is placed with its palm away from the camera, as the walker sees
their own hands.

| File | What happens | Length |
|---|---|---|
| `hands_wave.handlog` | Two raised open hands wave, each swinging 14 degrees about its elbow, 0.20 m below the palm (the right at 1.6 Hz, the left at 1.3 Hz), 0.45 m from the camera and 0.24 m apart. The head sways by up to 3 degrees of yaw, 1.5 degrees of pitch and 1 cm sideways, so the camera pose of each record matters | 5 s, 151 records |
| `hands_press.handlog` | A right hand points. Its index fingertip, on the line through (0.04, 0.02) m straight away from the camera, moves from 0.40 m to 0.52 m and back three times (0.45 s forward, 0.20 s held, 0.45 s back, 0.40 s rest), with 0.5 s of rest before the first press and after the last cycle. The palm is about 0.10 m behind the tip, so it is nearer than LiDAR's 0.3 m for part of every press | 5.5 s, 166 records |
| `hands_pinch.handlog` | A right hand raised 0.42 m from the camera: the thumb and the index close on each other three times (0.20 s to close, 0.45 s held with the tips touching, 0.20 s to open, then rest, one pinch every 1.5 s), with 0.5 s before the first and after the last cycle. The gap between the tips over the palm's width, the design's pinch ratio, falls from more than 0.8 to 0 | 5.5 s, 166 records |

They are the output of `SampleHands` in `ATLAS/Assets/Atlas/Tests/Hands/HandsLogFixtures.cs`, and the test
`HandsLogTests.Hands_Log_FileEqualsGenerator` compares each file with it byte for byte (the generator rounds the joints
and the camera to a micrometre, so another machine's sines and cosines cannot change a byte). The log tests and the
tests of the sources read them. To regenerate them after a change to the generator or to `HandModel`:

```sh
ATLAS_WRITE_RECORDINGS=1 python3 tools/headless/verify.py test Atlas.Hands.Tests --filter "FullyQualifiedName~HandsLogTests"
```
